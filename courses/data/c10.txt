TITLE|Moving Minds
SUBTITLE|The Psychology of Influence, Persuasion, and Sales — from the First Hello to the Cast Vision
ACCENT|#8c4f2b
ACCENTSOFT|#f4ebe3
DESC|A working education in how people are moved: the decision machinery under every yes, the classic principles of influence, the battle for the frame, why and when people actually buy, and the full craft of selling from cold open to signed deal. Then the course widens: persuading across political divides, selling an idea to co-workers, leading change, and casting a vision to a family or a church. Built on the serious research — Kahneman, Cialdini, Rackham, Voss, Haidt — and on the great practitioners, with the strongest objections to each technique engaged rather than avoided. House style: every lesson ends with The One Move, a single concrete application to use that week. Closing conviction, argued not assumed: the best persuaders play the long game, and integrity is not the enemy of influence but its foundation.
